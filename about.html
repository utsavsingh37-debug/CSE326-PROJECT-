The idea behind the interface

Less wandering.
More meaningful work.

StudySpot is a student-centred concept designed to make choosing a campus study environment easier and more intentional.

The problem

Finding a seat shouldn't take the whole break.

On a busy campus, students may not know which spaces are quiet, which support group work or where basic facilities are available. This uncertainty can waste time and interrupt a planned study session.

Our proposed approach

  • Organise spaces by study purpose
  • Show useful facilities at a glance
  • Let students filter by their priorities
  • Encourage thoughtful use of shared spaces
Project objectives

What this project demonstrates.

Honest project scope

What works — and what needs a backend.

Included in this version

  • Four connected responsive pages
  • CSS-only category filters
  • Sample space directory and details
  • Study tips and project explanation

Future development

  • Live occupancy and opening hours
  • Student-submitted space updates
  • Saved favourites and accounts
  • Real campus map and location data

All locations and facility descriptions in this demonstration are sample content, not verified campus listings. HTML and CSS alone cannot store submissions, authenticate users or fetch live availability.
